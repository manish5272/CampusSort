Campus Skill & Job Recommendation Platform

CampusSort is a campus recruitment platform designed to connect students with relevant job opportunities based on their skills, qualifications, and career interests.

Platform Features

Student Profiles

Students can maintain their academic and skill information.

Skill Management

Students can record and manage their technical skills.

Job Listings

Recruitment opportunities can be displayed in one centralized platform.

Skill Matching

Job requirements can be compared with student skills to identify suitable opportunities.
